# Audit Protocol & Methodology

## Verification Tiers

| Tier | Definition | Publication Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VERIFIED** | Confirmed vs. primary government source | Permitted |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Lacks direct primary source verification | Bracketed notation |
| **NOT FOUND** | Searched but not located | Explicitly stated |

## Statistical Constraints
- **Descriptive Analytics Only**: Counts, percentages, ranges, weighted averages
- **Prohibited**: Meta-analytic pooling, heterogeneity testing, synthetic error margins

## Geographical Mapping Mandate
Regional grouping derives exclusively from **CMS Geographic Rating Area (GRA) Crosswalk**. County matching performed on string county name, not FIPS codes.
