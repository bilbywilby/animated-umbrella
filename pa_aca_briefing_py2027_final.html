CMS Risk-Adjustment Audit Validation

Cross-referencing CMS BY2025 risk-adjustment summary data verifies the morbidity basis for PID rate decisions.

Above-Request Approvals Justified

Keystone East (+$41.4M), QCC (+$3.7M), and HPP (+$24.8M) were approved above initial requests due to confirmed higher member morbidity and sicker risk pools.

Ambetter Transfer Volume

Ambetter (HIOS 86199) is the largest single risk-adjustment recipient in PA at +$61.1M, reflecting high medical loss experience.

Vertical Integration vs. Independent Carriers

Market data indicates integrated health systems (e.g., UPMC, Geisinger) maintain pricing stability relative to independent regional carriers.

Integrated System Mechanics

Internal clinical cost capture and provider alignment buffer integrated networks against external fee schedule inflation.

Independent Carrier Burden

Non-integrated plans face direct out-of-network costs and higher morbidity pressure, forcing higher rate increases.

Geographic Variance Across PA Rating Areas

Rate increases vary substantially by geographic rating area (RA1 through RA9).

Lehigh Valley (RA 6): High competition with 10 carriers present, but elevated premium spikes due to local hospital system pricing dynamics.

Rural West & North (RA 1, RA 2): Concentrated carrier choice driven primarily by Highmark and UPMC.

The $600M Reinsurance Funding Deficit

Pennsylvania's state-based reinsurance program faces structural unfunded liabilities for future plan years.

Market Implications

  • Unfunded gaps increase risk premiums directly in baseline rate filings.
  • Hospitals and health systems absorb uncompensated care as subsidy margins compress.

Federal Regulatory Changes Effective 2027

APTC / CSR Eligibility Shifts
