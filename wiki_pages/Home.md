# Pennsylvania Health Insurance Audit Protocol (PHIAP) Wiki

Welcome to the official technical documentation for the **Pennsylvania Health Insurance Audit (PY2027)**.

## Project Scope
Compliance audit of Pennsylvania's individual health insurance market (Pennie ACA exchange) and carrier rate filings with PID.

### Core Audit Questions
1. **Rate Approvals**: Requested vs. approved rate adjustments for PY2027 across 9 CMS rating areas
2. **Enrollment Attrition**: Reconciliation of Pennie enrollment figures
3. **Open Enrollment Compliance**: Nov 1, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027 window
4. **Consumer Protections**: Act 146 of 2022, Article XXI, No Surprises Act
5. **Cost-Sharing Limits**: State-level legislative proposals
6. **Rating Area Assignment**: CMS crosswalk mapping for 67 PA counties
